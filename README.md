## About Me

I'm a **Data &amp; AI Engineer** based in Montreal, Canada, currently working as a **Junior Data Engineer at PwC Canada** while completing my **Master of Engineering in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering at Concordia University**.

My journey began in robotics, embedded systems, and computer vision research, where I built intelligent automation and deep learning prototypes. That foundation led me into **data engineering at Cognizant**, where I progressed from Data Engineer Intern to Associate Data Engineer designing ETL/ELT pipelines on Microsoft Azure, and later into a **Data Scientist** role delivering machine learning solutions on AWS.

Today I work at the intersection of **cloud, data engineering, and applied AI** — building distributed Spark pipelines, cloud data warehouses, and deployed, explainable models that help teams make better decisions.

- 🔭 Currently building an **end-to-end Data &amp; AI Platform**, alongside autonomous robotics, drone, and driving systems
- 🌱 Deepening my expertise in **distributed systems**, **MLOps**, and **cloud architecture**
- 🎓 **M.Eng, Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering** — Concordia University, Montreal *(2025 – 2027)*
- 🎓 **B.Tech, Electronics &amp; Communication Engineering** — Sreenidhi Institute of Science and Technology *(2018 – 2022)*
- 📜 **3× Microsoft Certified** — Azure Fundamentals · Azure Data Fundamentals · Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate
- 🏆 **Bronze Award**, E-NNOVATE Virtual Innovation Fair 2020 — for *CHAUSER*, a smart automation prototype
- 📄 Published research on **facial emotion detection using CNNs** — IRJMETS, Volume 4, Issue 03 (2022)
- 👥 Former **Vice President – External**, Engineering &amp; Computer Science Graduate Association (ECSGA), Concordia University
- 💬 Ask me about **Data Engineering, Apache Spark, Azure, AWS, and Machine Learning**
